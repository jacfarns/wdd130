History

Here at YOLO Rafting company we have been rafting since we were 12 years old. From our first rafting trip we fell in love with the river. 10 years ago an opportunity to buy a rafting company landed right in our laps it was a YOLO (You Only Live Once) opportunity so we seized the opportunity. We were blessed with a YOLO opportunity and our goal is to provide each of our guest with a YOLO expereince with every visit. We have been ceritfied in every possible way to make sure your trip is as safe as possible. For 10 years we have been making YOLO opportunities safe but someting you will always remember!
